# Changelog

## 2026-11 — `falsification/` (new)
Manuscript, code, data and logs of the falsification study *Mock controls show that host-dependent
gravitational kernels reduce to the baryon fraction: a falsification of the RGD kernel from galaxies
to clusters*. Independent of `v5/`; nothing outside `falsification/` was changed except this file
and the status note at the top of `README.md`.

- **Circularity control.** Mocks with only the baryon fraction f_bar(M) reproduce the RGD (v4)
  exponent law n(ξ)=½+0.4068ξ (r=0.978, A=0.411): the total-mass host variable ξ is circular.
- **Baryonic host variable ξ_b** tested on 23 groups (Sun+2009) and 12 X-COP clusters against two
  null models and 200 ΛCDM mock replicas: the gain over the nulls is indistinguishable from the
  mocks (p=0.45 under G13); under B14 it loses.
- **Galaxies.** λ with free M/L = 0.933±0.011; the RAR shape is preferred (Δχ²=+166).
  A_gal ≈ −9 against A_cl ≈ +2.5: a single linear law is falsified (Δχ²=+73).
- **BCG excess.** At equal (g_b, α_b) CLASH BCGs have 2.4× the g_obs of SPARC spirals (11.5σ); no
  local law F(g_b, Φ_b) fits both; the kernel would need n≈2.3–2.5, which admits no QUMOND-type action (n ≤ 1).
- **H0.** 70 → 67.4 km/s/Mpc changes results by < 2 %.
